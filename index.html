The VESTARA layer

A digital identity for every asset.

VESTARA aims to give physical assets persistent digital identities supported by organized, transparent and verifiable records.

⌁

Asset Identity

Every registered asset receives a unique digital identity that can be referenced throughout its lifecycle.

◌

Verifiable History

Ownership events, documents, inspections and relevant records can be organized into a transparent asset history.

◇

Digital Ownership

Connect supported ownership records and eligible assets to blockchain-based infrastructure over time.

A connected lifecycle

From physical object to trusted record.

A proposed workflow for establishing context, evidence and continuity around real-world value.

Verify Its Records

Review evidence through an applicable verification process.

04 / CONNECT

Connect to Infrastructure

Trust boundary

Evidence is specific.

A verified record does not automatically guarantee legal ownership, physical condition, authenticity beyond the evidence reviewed, absence of liens or the accuracy of unverified information.
